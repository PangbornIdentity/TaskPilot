# CVS — Reconciliation Ledger (Phase P2)

> The human-reviewable record of every cross-source **conflict** found while reconciling the four
> sources (tests, code, requirement records, and the prose docs) against the **actual code**. Produced
> in P2; resolved by you in **P6** (the agent will walk you through it ~5 rows at a time, proposing a
> Decision for each). This is one of the two mandatory human stops.
>
> **Scope:** this ledger holds *source disagreements*. Pure *code bugs* (no source conflict) live
> separately in [`DEFECTS.md`](./DEFECTS.md) (D-001…D-006) — review both. Some rows cross-reference a
> `D-`/`Q-` item where a conflict and a defect describe the same root cause.
>
> **How to use (P6):** fill the last three columns per row —
> `Decision` ∈ {Accept, Reject, Edit, Defer}; `Edited_Statement` (the exact new requirement statement if
> Accept/Edit rewrites a record); `Ratified` ∈ {Y, N}. `cvs apply-ledger` will then auto-apply the
> mechanical rows (Edit / Accept-with-Edited_Statement → rewrite the record; Ratified=Y → flip status),
> and list fix-code / fix-doc / create-new rows as manual follow-up.
>
> **Nothing in the requirement records was edited in P2.** Auto-added `provenance: doc` records (the
> "missing"/aspirational rows) were created as `unratified`, `tests: []`, and marked aspirational in
> their body — they enter the gap baseline; ratifying them here confirms them as intended-but-unbuilt.
>
> Created 2026-06-14 (P2). Columns follow Playbook Appendix B.
